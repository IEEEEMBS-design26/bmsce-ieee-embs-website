About BMSCE IEEE EMBS

Connecting engineering, medicine and biology to shape the future of healthcare.

Who We Are

BMSCE IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society is a student chapter that brings together students interested in engineering, medicine, biology and healthcare technology.

Through technical activities, workshops, collaborative learning and events, we encourage students to explore innovative ideas and discover how engineering can contribute to healthcare.

✦

Engineering Meets Healthcare

Exploring ideas where technology and life sciences come together to create meaningful impact.

◎

Our Mission

To encourage interdisciplinary learning, innovation and collaboration in biomedical engineering and healthcare technology.

✧

Our Vision

To build an active student community that explores emerging technologies and contributes to the future of medicine and biology.
